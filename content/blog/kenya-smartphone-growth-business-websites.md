Kenya had **52.3 million smartphones connected to mobile networks at the end of June 2026**, up 48% in two years, according to the Communications Authority of Kenya (CA). Over the same period, feature phones fell by 3.5 million. For most Kenyan businesses, that means the first impression a customer gets is now formed on a phone screen, often on mobile data.

## What the CA data shows

The CA's latest quarterly report, for April to June 2026, counts 52,262,565 connected smartphones. That is up 4.2% on the previous quarter, 19.5% on a year earlier and 48.4% on June 2024. Over the same two years, connected feature phones fell from 30.9 million to 27.4 million.

The result is a quiet turning point. In June 2024, smartphones made up 53% of connected phones. By June 2026, they made up 66%.

Other figures in the same report point the same way:

- **Mobile data subscriptions** reached 64.3 million, and 85.5% of them were on mobile broadband (3G, 4G or 5G).
- **Mobile money subscriptions** grew 13.2% over the year to 54.0 million.
- **Mobile money agents** have grown too. The Central Bank of Kenya counted 563,605 active agents in August 2026, up from 451,157 a year earlier.

### What these numbers don't tell you

A connected smartphone is a device on a network, not a person. Many Kenyans carry two phones or two SIMs. The CA puts connected devices at 79.7 million, a penetration rate of 149.4%, which is more devices than people. So "52 million smartphones" does not mean 52 million smartphone owners.

The CA also revises figures. We used the latest published number for every quarter. For example, the March 2024 smartphone count was revised from 34.5 million to 34.1 million in the following report. The direction of the trend is not affected.

## Why smartphone use is climbing

The CA attributes the growth to the continued expansion of 4G and 5G networks. Its reports don't measure other likely factors, such as handset prices or financing, so we won't put numbers on them.

What the data does show clearly is that growth is steady, not a one-off spike. Smartphone connections rose in every quarter shown in the chart. The biggest jumps came in the October–December quarters of both 2024 (+10.9%) and 2025 (+9.1%). That may reflect holiday buying, but the reports don't break it down.

## What it means for your business

Our view: if most of your customers now meet you on a phone, your website should be judged by how it works on an ordinary Android handset on mobile data, not on a laptop in the office. Here are five places to start.

1. **Test on a real mid-range phone, on mobile data.** Open your main service page with Wi-Fi switched off. If the page takes long enough to make you impatient, your customers feel it too. Large images and auto-playing video are the usual causes.
2. **Make the first screen do the selling.** Without scrolling, a visitor should see what you offer, where you serve, and how to reach you. Put prices or a price range there if you can. Kenyan buyers ask "how much?" early.
3. **Make WhatsApp and calls one tap away.** Use a click-to-chat link with a short pre-filled message ("Hi, I'm interested in…"), and a tap-to-call number. If you use [WhatsApp automation](/services/whatsapp-email-crm-automation), make sure a human picks up promptly.
4. **Explain how to pay.** If you accept M-Pesa, show your Till or Paybill number and what happens after payment. Never ask customers to type payment details into a contact form.
5. **Keep forms short.** Ask only for what you need to reply: name, phone and the service. Every extra field loses some people on a small screen.

### Who should act first

The shift matters most where buyers decide quickly and on the move. Think of restaurants, salons, hotels and tour operators, home services such as plumbing or solar installation, and shops that sell through Instagram or TikTok. For these businesses, a customer often finds you, checks your prices and messages you within a few minutes, all on one phone.

Businesses that sell mainly to other businesses are less exposed, since many of their buyers still research at a desk. But the person who first finds you is often checking on a phone between meetings, so the first screen still counts.

### Measure what changes

Before you change anything, note your current numbers in Google Analytics: mobile visits, and how many become WhatsApp clicks, calls or form submissions. Compare again after a month. Track the share of traffic from mobile on your own site. Public estimates of mobile versus desktop traffic for Kenya swing sharply month to month, so your own data is the better guide.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many smartphones are there in Kenya in 2026?

As of 30 June 2026, the CA counted 52,262,565 smartphones connected to mobile networks in Kenya, up from 43.8 million a year earlier.

### Does that mean 52 million Kenyans own a smartphone?

No. The figure counts connected devices, and many people use more than one. The CA's total of 79.7 million connected devices is higher than Kenya's population.

### Should my business build an app instead of improving its website?

Usually not first. Most customers find a business through search, social media or WhatsApp and land on a web page. Improve that experience first. An app makes sense when customers repeat a task often, such as ordering or booking every week.
